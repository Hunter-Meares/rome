Features

Rome: The Eternal City is a world being actively built as a genuine recreation of the Roman Empire and the neighbors, allies, and enemies that surrounded it - and it includes features you won't find in many other MUDs.

Race & Class

Six races and eight classes - forty-eight real combinations, and race and class both genuinely change how your character plays, not just how they look.

Four Core Stats

Virtus, Agilitas, Ingenium, and Vigor drive real combat math - melee damage, accuracy, dodge, spell power, and max HP/MP all trace back to these four numbers, not hidden behind the scenes.

Race Shapes the Build

A Minotaur and a Harpy playing the same class feel different in a fight - each race pushes your stats in a different direction from the moment you're created.

Eight Distinct Classes

From the shield-wall discipline of a Legionary to the curse-laced rituals of a Haruspex to the battlefield healing of a Medicus, every class has its own full kit of spells or skills. See them all on the Classes and Skills & Spells pages.

On the horizon: each race's unique signature ability - already described on the Races page - is being built out into a real, innate mechanic, not just flavor text on top of the stat bonuses.

A Real Combat System

This isn't roll-a-die-and-hope combat. Rome runs on a full turn-based system with initiative order, wielded weapons, worn armor, status conditions, usable items, and a real spellbook - all tracked live with an HP, MP, and SP prompt that updates after every action. Gear, class training, and stamina all genuinely matter, and opponents make real decisions on their turn instead of standing still and waiting to be hit.

Character Progression

A real leveling system tracks your growth from your first fight onward, with a full character sheet and a rank title that reflects exactly how far you've come.

Rank Titles

From Novice to Legend, your rank is visible to everyone who checks who's online - a permanent, public marker of everything you've survived to get there.

Real Achievements

From your first kill to escaping the Colosseum itself, real milestones are tracked and announced the moment you earn them - visible, permanent proof of what you've actually done.

Real Stakes

Fleeing a losing fight isn't guaranteed - disengaging can fail, costing you a turn while still fully exposed. Combat here asks for real judgment, not just a panic button.

Death, Companions & Camaraderie

Death Has Real Weight

Newer characters get a second chance - the gods aren't done with them yet. Seasoned characters face the Underworld itself: a real, explorable land of the dead with two genuine ways back - solve a riddle yourself, or find a healer willing to cross over and bring you home.

Summon a Companion

Certain classes can call a spirit, familiar, or beast to fight at their side, each with its own signature trick in battle - and real care taken so it's never simply abandoned mid-fight if you flee or fall.

Stronger Together

A real party system rewards grouping up with a genuine XP bonus for fighting as a team, not just a shared kill count.

Dynamic Divine Influence

The gods are not lore you read once and forget. They bless, curse, and interfere - and divine favor can genuinely change a character's standing in the world. Meet the pantheon on the Gods page and read how it all began on the Mythology page.

Factions, Cults & Divine Devotion

Eight Orders and Cults already have a real place in the world, from the disciplined ranks of the Imperial Legion to the banned, ecstatic rites of the Cult of Bacchus. See who's who on the Factions page.

Real, Weighty Membership

Joining a faction or cult in-game is a genuine, permanent commitment, not a cosmetic label - once you're in, only that faction's own leader (or a god) can release you. Membership grants its own small set of abilities on top of whatever your race and class already give you.

Devotion to the Gods

Beyond Orders and Cults, you can devote yourself directly to one of the Twelve. Real devotion brings real, tangible favor - Mars sharpens a devotee's blade, Mercury sweetens a deal, Apollo's touch heals deeper, and Pluto's faithful face a lighter hand from death itself.

On the Horizon: More Gods, More Blessings

Not every one of the Twelve rewards devotion with a concrete mechanical blessing yet - expanding that roster of real, tangible favor is ongoing.

The World: Recreating Rome

Rome isn't a handful of static rooms - it's a real place, built to be walked through, not just described.

The Colosseum, in Full

Not one tutorial room - a genuine multi-level landmark, from the tunnels and animal dens of the Hypogeum beneath the sand, up through every tier of seating - senators closest to the action, the poorest citizens highest and most exposed - all the way to the awning-riggers' perch at the very top.

A Work in Progress, by Design

Beyond the Colosseum, the ambition is the whole city: the Forum Romanum and every other major landmark of ancient Rome, built district by district rather than faked with a handful of placeholder rooms. This is genuinely still underway - what exists today is the foundation, not the finished city.

Built to Last Beyond Rome

The long-term vision extends past Rome's own walls, to other great cities of antiquity - a foundation, not a finished product, shaped by what the people actually playing want built next.

Accessible By Design

No installs required to get started - play instantly through our web client, or connect with Mudlet, a MUSH client, or a mobile client if you'd rather have triggers, mapping, and macros. See every option on the Play Now page.

Already In Your Pocket

A real economy already lets you buy and sell gear, learn spells and skills from in-person trainers, and trade directly with other players.

On the Horizon

Crafting and a broader player-run marketplace are still on the roadmap, alongside expanded world systems. Features are added frequently through playtests and community feedback - what ships next is genuinely shaped by the people playing now.
