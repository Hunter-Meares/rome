Rome Stands at the Height of Its Power

Legions march the frontiers. Senators scheme beneath marble ceilings. And the gods - real, present, and rarely subtle - watch every move from Olympus. Rome: The Eternal City is a text-based multiplayer world where your choices actually shape the story, now in our Player Testing phase.

Live, Persistent World

A growing recreation of ancient Rome - the Forum, the Colosseum, the streets between - shaped by gods, politics, and every choice players make.

Deep Roleplay

Character, story, and community-driven events come first here - backed by a real turn-based combat system when the moment calls for steel instead of words.

Active Devs

Frequent updates, honest player testing, and a team that actually reads your feedback. Type bug or idea in-game any time.

Every Story Begins the Same Way - What Happens Next Is Yours

Every character wakes in the cells beneath the Colosseum, with nothing but wits and will. Maybe you were dragged there in chains, torn from a battlefield far from home. Maybe you volunteered, chasing a glory only the games can offer. Maybe Rome itself is stranger to you than the cell walls - pulled from a life, a forest, a people the Empire never touched. However you arrived, the path from here is entirely your own: fight your way to freedom in the games, or slip out unseen through the tunnels below.

A World Shaped by Politics and Power

Public Orders like the Imperial Legion and the Praetorian Order hold influence openly. Secret Cults - Mithras, the Orphic Mysteries, Hecate's covens - trade in power no senator would admit to needing. Nothing about the balance is fixed, and player action is exactly what tips it.
