About Rome: The Eternal City

The year is uncertain, but the age is not: Rome stands at the height of its power. Legions march the frontiers, senators scheme beneath marble ceilings, and the gods - real, present, and rarely subtle - watch every move from Olympus. This is the world you're stepping into.

We're currently in our Player Testing phase - the core game is complete and playable, and we're looking for real players to help us find what still needs work.

A City Worth Fighting For

Rome: The Eternal City is a MUD - a Multi-User Dungeon, the classic kind of text-based multiplayer world you play by reading and typing - built around one simple idea: a Roman Empire where the myths are true, and every player's choices actually matter to the story. New to MUDs? See What is a MUD?

A Living Recreation

Explore a growing, persistent recreation of ancient Rome itself - the Colosseum and its gladiator school, the Forum and Capitoline Hill, the crowded Subura, Trajan's Market, the Aventine and Palatine, and the sewers and buried palaces beneath - over eight hundred rooms so far, built room by room, district by district, with real history woven into every corner.

Beyond Rome's Walls

Rome is only the beginning. The road out through the Porta Flaminia already leads to a Germanic stronghold and a far-off coastal trading town, and the vision extends to the other great cities of antiquity - Athens, Sparta, Alexandria, and beyond - each with its own culture, politics, and reasons to resist or embrace Roman rule.

Gods Who Actually Show Up

The gods of Rome - fourteen in all - aren't lore you read about once. They're active presences who bless, curse, and occasionally walk among mortals directly, and you can devote yourself to one for real, tangible favor. Meet them on the Gods page.

Your Story Doesn't Start on a Throne

Every character's journey begins the same brutal way: as a captive, chained beneath the roar of the Colosseum crowd, with nothing to your name but your wits and your will. From there, the path is yours - fight your way to freedom in the games, or slip out through the shadows unseen. How you got into those cells in the first place is a story only you get to tell.

What you become afterward is entirely up to you. Rise as a Legionary holding the line in disciplined formation. Stalk the shadows as a Speculator, Rome's eyes and knives in enemy territory. Read the will of the gods as an Augur, or embrace the old ways as a Barbarian from the frontiers who owes Rome nothing. See the full list on the Classes and Skills & Spells pages, and the peoples who call this world home on the Races page.

A World Shaped By Its Players

Politics With Teeth

Public Orders like the Imperial Legion and the Praetorian Order hold power openly. Secret Cults trade in influence no senator would admit to needing. See who's really running things on the Factions page.

Deep, Collaborative Roleplay

This is a world built for storytellers as much as fighters - long-form roleplay, player-driven lore, and staff who actively run events rather than just moderate them. Not a fighter at all? A permanent pacifist path and a real crafting economy let you build a life in Rome without ever raising a weapon - see Features.

Built in the Open

Development happens through frequent playtests and direct community feedback. What gets built next is genuinely shaped by the people playing it.

Who We Are

A small, hands-on team of developers, writers, and roleplayers who enjoy collaborative storytelling and worldbuilding for its own sake. We're not chasing a AAA budget - we're building the game we'd want to play, one district, one god, one player story at a time.
