News & Updates

Rome is under active, ongoing development - here's a running log of what's actually shipped, most recent first. The same highlights show up in-game too: type motd or news once you're playing for the current headline.

Sep 25, 2026

Crafting arrives, plus a permanent pacifist path for storytellers

Rome now has a real crafting economy: gather, craft, sell. Timber and healing herbs turn up in the woods flanking the wilderness road, and iron ore lies deep in a newly expanded mine at the Germanic Stronghold. Two professions are open: Faber (smithing - a shortsword, a lorica, a war-spear) works at the forge in the Stronghold's Smithy, and Herbalist (a healing tonic, an antidote) brews at the apothecary's mortar in the Subura's Market Row. Just type craft and the recipe's name and the game finds your materials; recipes lists everything you could make. Each profession's first recipe is free to attempt, harder ones are learned from a trainer with learnrecipe, and your skill is described in words - bad, poor, average, superb, mastered - not numbers. Crafted goods pay gold and XP when sold, more the farther you are from Rome, with a bonus from merchants who deal in your kind of goods. See help crafting, help faber, and help herbalist.

Not here to fight? pacifism lets a character step out of combat for good: you can never attack, and can never be attacked, by another player or any creature, anywhere. It's a permanent, one-way choice - only a god can undo it - and you'll see exactly what it costs before you confirm. A pacifist lays down every weapon and piece of armor and can only wear clothing from then on; the tailor in the Forum sells robes and tunics, and will tell you honestly they won't stop a blade. Everything else stays open: escape the cells with sneak and solve, explore the whole city, take on the quests built on visiting and talking, and level through crafting. journey now points a pacifist toward the workshops instead of the arena, and spellinfo and skillinfo now say whether each spell or skill can be used in combat, outside it, or any time. See help pacifism.

Sep 20, 2026

The Amber Coast opens, plus row positioning, companion pets, and 80 socials

A big one. The Amber Coast is open: a whole new coastal Germanic trading town far north of the existing Stronghold, with a real harbor, four distinct warbands, a matriarchal war-leader, a tidal Sacred Isle, two new shops, and a genuine capstone at the edge of the known coast - every enemy there carries real weapons and armor.

Combat also got real front/back row positioning (row/position), with polearms and ranged weapons able to reach a protected back-row target and spells always reaching past the front line regardless of position. Level 10+? Buy a permanent companion pet (buypet, Ludus Entrance) that follows you everywhere and stays yours until it's defeated or dismissed - though it won't hand you free XP for its own kills anymore.

Also new: 80 single-word social commands, including smile, wave, and bow (help socials for the full list), resistible debuffs/buffs, colored spoken languages, and the Germanic Stronghold's warriors now talk and drop their own unique gear when defeated.

Sep 17, 2026

Closed the disengage/rest loop, retired Animate Dead

Wounded enemies you leave standing now heal back up over time instead of sitting at whatever HP you left them at forever, and a successful disengage/flee now costs a bit of XP progress - still far lighter than dying, but no longer free. Haruspex's Animate Dead is gone, replaced by Summon Fury: a real late-game companion upgrade.

Sep 16, 2026

Class rebalance pass, racial abilities, and a new compare command

A full role-audit across Augur, Medicus, and Haruspex to make sure each class's kit actually matches its own identity, plus several new spells and skills. Five races now have a real, usable signature ability (racial). Added compare for checking gear side by side, and fixed several sewer creatures that had somehow shipped with no real stats at all.

Sep 14, 2026

Live bugfix batch, Discord is open

Fixed five real combat bugs surfaced by player reports. Our Discord server is now live and linked in-game and across the site - come talk to the dev team directly.

Sep 13, 2026

Player Testing phase begins

Rome opened its doors for real player testing. Added roleplay/description help topics and real pet-lifecycle management for anyone running a summoned companion.

Sep 12, 2026

Equipping gear, simplified

Unified weapon and armor equipping into one consistent equip/unequip pair instead of separate commands per slot.

Sep 6, 2026

Arena Fighters get real gear, chargen gets a real ceremony

The Deeper Sands' Arena Fighters now wield real, mechanically-active weapons and armor, and drop real loot when defeated. Combat now shows clear wound-status feedback as a fight goes on. Character creation got a full aesthetic pass - in-world framing, Roman numerals, per-race and per-class colors, and a proper ceremonial ending.

Sep 5, 2026

Deeper Sands rebalanced to true endgame

The Deeper Sands is now real level 75+ endgame content, no longer competing with the sewers as "where to go next" right out of the Ludus. Added working doors, statues of the gods, a real map of Rome (ask the Forum bookseller), and a cleaner boxed stats display.

Sep 4, 2026

Beyond the walls: the road to Germania

Built Rome's first real city gate, the Porta Flaminia, and the wilderness road beyond it - the game's first stretch of real travel outside the city, with roaming danger along the way. At the road's end: the Germanic Stronghold, a full leveling zone. Also added a new-player tutorial and the recall command.

Sep 2, 2026

Religion & the quest framework

Mortal characters can now devote themselves to one of the pantheon's fourteen gods for real, ongoing benefits. Also shipped the full quest framework - one-time, narrative objectives from named NPCs, distinct from the bounty board's repeatable kills.

Sep 1, 2026

A major expansion of Rome itself

Opened the Aventine Hill, the Domus Aurea, the Library of Rome, and Campus Martius, plus a proper road out to the Pantheon. Built the bounty board. Movement now costs real stamina over distance, and death carries real weight - your resources fully empty until you're resurrected.

Aug 27, 2026

The Ludus weaponsmith opens

The game's first shop selling real combat gear - three tiers of weapons and armor, priced and balanced for a fresh Colosseum escapee.

Aug 24–26, 2026

Real gear finally has somewhere to go

Weapons and armor now scale properly with level, every class starts with real, fitting armor instead of just a weapon, and dropped items now decay on their own so the world doesn't fill up with clutter.

Aug 23, 2026

The Capitoline Hill and the Forum Romanum open

Rome proper opened for the first time beyond the Colosseum - the Capitoline's temples and Senate, and the full Forum Romanum. Also shipped the Cursus Divinorum god-tier system and the game's five learnable languages, each with its own real trainer.

This log starts with our push into Player Testing - Rome's been in active development for a good while before that, and every future update will keep landing here as we ship it.
